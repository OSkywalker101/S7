AI ETHICS AND SUSTAINABILITY
Course Code: CIE734 Credits: 3:0:0
Pre – requisites: Nil Contact Hours: 42L
Course Coordinator: Dr. Rakesh Kalshetty
Course Contents
Unit 1
Foundations of Artificial Intelligence and Ethics: Introduction to artificial intelligence and intelligent
systems; motivation for responsible artificial intelligence; foundations of AI; background of AI;
autonomy; adaptability; interaction.
● Pedagogy: Interactive Lectures and Classroom Discussions, Concepts based learning through
PPTs.
● Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S3050790125000075
Unit II
Ethical Decision-Making, Responsibility and Design for Values: Ethical decision-making in AI;
ethical theories; values; ethics in practice; implementing ethical reasoning; responsible research and
innovation; accountability; responsibility; transparency; and design for values.
● Pedagogy: Interactive Lectures and Classroom Discussions, Concepts based learning through
PPTs.
● Link: https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/e-learning/preview/noc26_cs145
Unit III
Trustworthy AI, Alignment, Moral Machines and Long-Term Ethics: Ethical action in AI systems;
approaches to ethical reasoning by AI; designing artificial moral agents; levels of ethical behavior;
ethical status of AI systems.
● Pedagogy: Interactive Lectures and Classroom Discussions, Concepts based learning through
PPTs.
● Link: https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-ethics-and-policy
Unit IV
Governance and Responsible AI in Practice: Ensuring Responsible AI in Practice; Governance for
Responsible AI; Codes of Conduct; Inclusion and Diversity; The AI Narrative.
● Pedagogy: Interactive Lectures and Classroom Discussions, Concepts based learning through
PPTs, Case Study, Demo and Student Presentation.
● Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-026-02910-4

AI, Society and Socio-Economic Impacts: AI and society; broader societal implications of AI; superintelligence; responsible AI in the social context; socio-economic impacts of AI; and public policy issues

● Pedagogy: Interactive Lectures and Classroom Discussions, Concepts based learning through

Course Outcomes (COs):
At the end of the course, the student will be able to:
1. Understand the foundations of artificial intelligence and the need for ethical and responsible AI
systems.(PO-1,2,3,5,PSO-1,2)
2. Analyze ethical theories, values, responsibility, fairness, accountability, and privacy in AI design
and deployment. (PO-1,2,3,5,PSO-1,2)
3. Examine governance, codes of conduct, inclusion, and responsible AI practices in real-world
systems. (PO-1,2,3,5,PSO-1,2,3)
4. Evaluate the societal and socio-economic implications of AI technologies. (PO-1,2,3,PSO-1,2,3)
5. Explore trustworthy AI, alignment, moral machines, and long-term ethical challenges in AI. (PO1,2,3,4,PSO-1,2,3)
